Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to build a website?

Most websites take 2-4 weeks to complete, depending on complexity and requirements. We keep you updated throughout the process.

Do I own my website?

Yes, absolutely. You own everything. We provide you with full access to the domain, hosting, and all files.

Can you help me with updates after launch?

Yes! We offer ongoing maintenance and support packages to keep your website fresh and secure.

Do you provide hosting?

We can recommend reliable hosting providers and help with the setup, or you can use your own.

Is the website mobile-friendly?

All our websites are fully responsive and optimized for mobile devices, tablets, and desktops.

What if I need to make changes?

We provide a period of revisions after launch. Beyond that, we offer affordable update services.
